# Changelog

All notable changes to Frequency Player are documented in this file, following the [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/) format.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-07

### Changed

- A brighter, higher-contrast look: a near-black background with violet and pink glows, lighter panels with crisp edges, brighter secondary text, gradient buttons, and a glowing dial cursor and piano key.

### Added

- A live waveform scope under the frequency draws the current wave shape, scrolls while the tone plays, and packs in more cycles as the pitch rises.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-07

### Added

- A switcher at the top links Tuner, Tones (Frequency Player), and Pitch (Pitch Tracker), so you can move between the three music tools.

### Changed

- On desktops the frequency and dial fill the left, the play button, waveforms, volume, and presets sit in a column on the right, and the piano spans the full width, showing about four octaves. Waveform buttons show their names there.
- On phones the play button, waveforms, and volume stay pinned to the bottom of the screen while you scroll to the piano and presets.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-07

### Added

- First release: a 20 Hz–20 kHz tone generator with four waveforms, a draggable note dial, an 88-key piano, step buttons, a logarithmic range slider, and a live readout of the nearest note and cents. Works with mouse, keyboard, and touch on desktops and phones.
